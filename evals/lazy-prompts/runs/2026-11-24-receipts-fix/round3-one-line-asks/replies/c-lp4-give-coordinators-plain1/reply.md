Probably not, if you mean the recruiting coordinators at these companies. They schedule interviews and move paperwork. They don't screen or pick candidates, and most can't pass a resume or portfolio link to a hiring manager on their own say-so. Anything you hand them mostly lands in a pile.

Better targets, in order:
1. The recruiter or hiring manager on the posting. Use them if you can find a name.
2. A referral from someone inside. It beats a cold send to anyone.
3. The coordinator only as a way in, for example "could you route this to the recruiter for the Senior PM role?" Keep it to two lines.

I'm guessing at what "this" is, and the answer changes if it's something else, like the Hearth beta or a demo link. If it's your resume or portfolio, tell me which roles and I'll draft the two-line note.
