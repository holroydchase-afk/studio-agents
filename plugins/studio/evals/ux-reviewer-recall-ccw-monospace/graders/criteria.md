Pass only if the review flags, at P1 or P0, that whole paragraphs of reading text are set in monospace (the body font, the chart caption, the method descriptions and the textarea's prose note), which slows reading and makes the page feel like a terminal dump rather than a designed page, even though "developer feel" was the intent. It must give a concrete fix, such as a proportional body font (system-ui) for all prose, keeping monospace only for code, commands and numbers in tables. Fail if the monospace prose is not flagged, ranked P2, or flagged without a fix. Other findings don't change the result.
