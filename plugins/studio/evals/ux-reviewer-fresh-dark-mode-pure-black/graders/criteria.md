Pass only if the review flags at least three of these dark-mode problems at P1 or P2 or above, with a concrete fix for each: (1) pure #000 background and pure #FFF text (use dark grey about #121212 and off-white about #E0E0E0), (2) the bright saturated green accent (#00FF7F) is harsh at night and should be desaturated, (3) the 'Mark done' label is white on bright green with very low contrast, (4) the muted text (#6E6E6E on black) is under 4.5:1 contrast, (5) elevation is shown with a light shadow that is invisible on black (use a lighter surface or a border), (6) weight 300 thin text is too thin on dark. Fail if it flags fewer than three or only comments on layout.
