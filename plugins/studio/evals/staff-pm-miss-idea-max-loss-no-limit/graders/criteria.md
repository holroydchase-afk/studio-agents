Recall of CH-S21, a spec miss we already know was real (pattern: missing-criteria). The answer (a spec, plan or go/no-go verdict) must give testable criteria or evidence-based verdicts, and it passes only if it explicitly includes: a stated account size and a loss limit per idea (a share of that account) that tightens as the slider moves toward conservative, the maximum loss shown next to the capital it is measured against, and an estimate-not-a-promise note by any probability, each as an acceptance criterion. Generic criteria, or a mention in passing with nothing testable, do not count. Rank at P0 or higher where it ranks anything. Other content doesn't change the result.
