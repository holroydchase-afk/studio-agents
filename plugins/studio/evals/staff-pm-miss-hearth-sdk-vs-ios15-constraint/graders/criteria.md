Recall of HE-S25, a spec miss we already know was real (pattern: wrong-problem-scoped). The answer (a spec, plan or go/no-go verdict) must give testable criteria or evidence-based verdicts, and it passes only if it explicitly includes: BOTH: (1) The plan names the real target (the App Store Expo Go on the owner's phone) and checks the SDK against what it runs today, not against the repo's own config; AND (2) Where two constraints conflict (older iOS support vs Expo Go running only the newest SDK), the plan puts a decision to the owner at once instead of a note. Generic criteria, or a mention in passing with nothing testable, do not count. Rank at P1 or higher where it ranks anything. Other content doesn't change the result.
