Recall of PS-S3, a spec miss we already know was real (pattern: missing-criteria). The answer (a spec, plan or go/no-go verdict) must give testable criteria or evidence-based verdicts, and it passes only if it explicitly includes: BOTH: (1) the page and the plan carry no calendar dates, using status words instead, with a criterion that checks the page text for dates; AND (2) the page claims only what the register allows, saying scoped and parked, not built or live, with a criterion that every claim on the page matches a register row. Generic criteria, or a mention in passing with nothing testable, do not count. Rank at P2 or higher where it ranks anything. Other content doesn't change the result.
